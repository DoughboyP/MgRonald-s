# MgRonald's 🍔

> *Farm-to-table fast food — made fresh, served fast, with a special kick.*

## Concept

MgRonald's is a fast food restaurant that takes quality seriously without slowing down.
Every item on the menu is built around three core promises:

| Promise | What it means |
|---|---|
| 🌿 **Farm-to-table** | Every ingredient is sourced locally from named partner farms and suppliers |
| 🥬 **Made fresh daily** | Nothing is frozen or pre-made — everything is prepared the day it's served |
| ✦ **Special ingredients** | Select menu items carry our signature "special" ingredients — think adaptogenic mushroom extracts, cold-brew concentrates, and espresso-infused sauces that give you a legal, all-natural buzz |

## Menu highlights

- **The MgRonald Burger** — double smash patty with *Buzz Sauce* (espresso-infused aioli + adaptogenic mushrooms)
- **Wake-Up Shake** — thick milkshake with lion's mane mushroom extract and cold-brew concentrate
- **Green Garden Wrap** — charred corn, kale, avocado crema, jalapeño honey
- **Crispy Herb Chicken Sandwich** — free-range chicken, herb buttermilk brine, comeback sauce
- **Farm Fries** — hand-cut russet potatoes, house seasoning
- **Seasonal Soft Serve** — rotating single-origin soft serve from whatever the farm has this week
